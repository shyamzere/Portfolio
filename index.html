Synapic, AI study assistant

2026

The problem

Students lose hours turning lecture notes into flashcards and quizzes before they can even start studying.

What I built

Paste notes in, get flashcards, quizzes and summaries back. React front end, FastAPI back end, Groq LLM, Supabase for auth and saved decks, CI/CD on Vercel and Render.

The result

Live with 50+ active users. Generation takes under three seconds and manual prep time dropped by over 70%.
